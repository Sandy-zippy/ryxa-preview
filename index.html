All questions
Who can buy from RYXA Labs?

Researchers, laboratories, institutions and businesses aged 19 or older who will use our products for laboratory research only. You confirm this at checkout.

Can you tell me how to dose or reconstitute a product?

No. We do not give dosing, administration or preparation advice, and our team cannot answer these questions. Please refer to published literature and your institution's protocols for laboratory handling.

How do I know what's in the vial?

Every lot is tested by an independent laboratory for purity (HPLC) and identity (mass spectrometry). Enter the lot number from your label in our COA library to see its report.

When will my order ship?

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Pacific on a business day ship within 1 to 2 business days, in insulated packaging. We ship Monday to Thursday only, so parcels don't sit in a depot over the weekend.

What payment methods do you accept?

Interac e-Transfer and crypto (Bitcoin and USDT), plus invoicing for approved institutional accounts. Prices are in CAD.

Can I cancel or return an order?

You can cancel free of charge before it ships. Because products are temperature-sensitive, we can't accept returns, but we replace or refund damaged, incorrect, lost or out-of-specification orders.

RYXA Labs products are research chemicals sold for laboratory use only. They are not drugs, not approved by Health Canada, and not for human or veterinary use. We do not give dosing or preparation advice.
