Health Canada, in plain words.

Health Canada said more about peptides in 2026 than in any year before. You should hear it from us before you order.

  1. April 2026

    A public advisory against injecting peptides bought online.

    It named BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu and others, and warned that unauthorized products can cause serious harm. Read the advisory

  2. November 2026

    Bacteria found in unauthorized injectable peptides.

    Health Canada tested seized products, found contamination and told people not to use them. A purity certificate does not test for bacteria. Read the warning

  3. Where we stand

    Our products are research materials, not medicines.

    None is authorized by Health Canada for human or animal use. We publish no dosing advice and make no health claims. If you want a peptide for your health, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.

Who can buy from RYXA Labs?

Researchers, laboratories, institutions and businesses aged 19 or older who will use our products for laboratory research only. You confirm this at checkout.

What form do products come in?

Freeze-dried powder in sealed glass vials, labelled with the compound, amount, lot number and storage conditions.

What purity do you guarantee?

We only release lots at 98% purity or higher by HPLC. Members can open the certificate for the lot they received.

How do I see a certificate of analysis?

Create a free member account. Once we approve it, you can open both certificates for any lot.

When will my order ship?

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Pacific on a business day ship within one to two business days. We ship Monday to Thursday only.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer or crypto. Approved institutional accounts can pay by invoice. Prices are in Canadian dollars.

Can I cancel or return an order?

You can cancel free of charge before it ships. We cannot accept returns, but we replace or refund damaged, incorrect, lost or out-of-specification orders.

Can you tell me how to dose or use a product?

No. Our products are for laboratory research only, and we do not give dosing, administration or preparation advice.
