Who can buy from RYXA Labs?

Adults 19 and over buying for laboratory research, shipping to a Canadian address. Every order carries a research-use attestation at checkout.

How do I know what’s in the vial?

Every vial carries a lot number. Enter it at the top of this page to open that lot’s certificate of analysis: HPLC purity, mass-spec identity, the lab and the test date.

When will my order ship?

Monday to Thursday. Orders placed before 2 PM Pacific ship the same day; later orders ship on the next ship day.

Can you tell me how to dose or reconstitute a product?

No. Our products are for laboratory research use only, and we do not give dosing, administration or preparation advice.

Do you offer institutional accounts?

Yes: purchase orders, invoicing, net-30 for approved accounts and lot reservation.
