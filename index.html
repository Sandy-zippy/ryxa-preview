Nothing ships until its lot has a public report.

Five things are true of every vial we sell. Each one is printed, measured or published, so you can check it.

  • Sealed glass

    Crimp-sealed vial with a flip-off cap. If the seal is broken, we replace it.

  • Net peptide, reported

    Purity says how much of the peptide is the right molecule. Net peptide says how much of the powder is peptide. We publish both.

  • Lot on the back

    Turn the vial: LOT and EXP match a public report that names the lab and test date. Stock is retested each quarter.

  • Identity on every lot

    Mass spectrometry confirms the molecule named on the label, lot by lot.

  • One code, everywhere

    RX-01 on the vial, the carton and the catalogue. Same family, same colour.
