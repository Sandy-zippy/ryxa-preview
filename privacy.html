Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RYXA Labs, operating as RYXA Labs ("we", "us"), collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit www.ryxalabs.com or buy from us.

1. Information we collect

Type Examples Source
Identity and contact Name, email, phone, billing and shipping address You, at checkout or account sign-up
Institution details Institution or company name, role You, if provided
Order records Products, lot numbers, order history, research-use and age confirmations with time stamp and IP address Created when you order

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 19.

2. How we use it

  • To process, ship and support orders

  • To verify eligibility and keep records of research-use confirmations

  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to meet legal, tax and regulatory duties

  • To run, secure and improve the Site

  • To send marketing emails, only with your consent; you can unsubscribe at any time

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, such as payment processors, shipping carriers, website hosting, email and analytics providers, and professional advisers, under contracts that protect it. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies (for example, the cart and age confirmation) and privacy-friendly analytics to understand how the Site is used. We do not use advertising cookies. You can clear or block cookies in your browser.

5. Where your information is stored

Our providers may store information outside your province, state or country, including in COUNTRIES. When that happens it may be subject to the laws of that place.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order and attestation records for [7] years for legal, tax and compliance purposes, and other information only as long as needed for the purposes above.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is completely secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain uses. Email info@ryxalabs.com. We will respond within the time the law requires. You may also complain to your privacy regulator.

9. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above.
