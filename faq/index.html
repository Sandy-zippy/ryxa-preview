FAQ

Products and use

Who can buy from RYXA Labs? Researchers, laboratories, institutions and businesses aged 19 or older who will use our products for laboratory research only. You confirm this at checkout.

Can I use these products on myself or give them to a person or animal? No. All RYXA Labs products are for laboratory research use only. They are not for human or veterinary use, and not for consumption.

Can you tell me how to dose or reconstitute a product? No. We do not give dosing, administration or preparation advice, and our team cannot answer these questions. Please refer to published literature and your institution’s protocols for laboratory handling.

What form do products come in? Lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder in sealed glass vials, labelled with the compound, amount, lot number and storage conditions.

Testing and quality

How do I know what’s in the vial? Every lot is tested by an independent laboratory for purity (HPLC) and identity (mass spectrometry). Scan the QR code on your vial, or search the lot number in our COA Library.

What purity do you guarantee? Each product page shows the tested purity for the current lot.

What if a lot doesn’t match its COA? Contact us within 30 days of delivery with an independent lab report. We’ll replace it, refund it, or reimburse reasonable testing costs, as set out in our Refund & Return Policy.

Storage

How should I store products? As shown on the label and product page. Most lyophilized peptides are kept at 2–8 °C short term or −20 °C long term, dry and away from light.

Orders and shipping

Where do you ship? Within Canada. See our Shipping Policy for details.

When will my order ship? Orders placed before 2:00 PM Pacific on a business day ship within 1–2 business days. We ship Monday to Thursday only, so parcels don’t sit in a depot over the weekend.

How are products packed? In protective packaging, with cold packs or a temperature indicator when the season and route need it.

Can I cancel or return an order? You can cancel free of charge before it ships. Because products are temperature-sensitive, we can’t accept returns, but we replace or refund damaged, incorrect, lost or out-of-specification orders. See our Refund & Return Policy.

Accounts and payment

What payment methods do you accept? Interac e-Transfer, plus invoicing for approved institutional accounts. Prices are in CAD.

Do you offer institutional accounts or bulk pricing? Yes. See Wholesale & Institutional Accounts.
